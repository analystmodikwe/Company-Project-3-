# Riverside Community Hub — Staff Handover Guide

This guide explains how Riverside staff and admins use the platform
day-to-day. No technical background needed.

## Logging in

1. Go to the site's web address.
2. Click **Log in** in the top-right corner.
3. Enter your email and password.

If you don't have an account yet, click **Need an account? Sign up** and
fill in your name, email, and a password. New accounts start as regular
members — ask an admin to upgrade your account to Staff if you need
approval/reporting access (see "Promoting a staff member" below).

## Approving or rejecting a booking

1. Log in with a Staff or Admin account.
2. Click **Admin** in the top navigation.
3. You'll land on the **Pending Bookings** tab — this lists every
   booking request waiting on a decision, oldest first.
4. Each row shows the facility, the member's name, and the requested
   date/time.
5. Click **Approve** or **Reject**.
   - The member sees the updated status immediately on their own
     "My Bookings" page.
   - The member also receives a notification.
6. The booking disappears from the pending list once you've decided.

**Note:** the system will not let two people book the same room or
equipment for an overlapping time — this is enforced automatically, so
you never need to manually check for double-bookings.

## Viewing members

1. From the Admin page, click the **Members** tab.
2. You'll see every registered member: name, role, membership tier, and
   join date.

## Viewing reports

1. From the Admin page, click the **Reports** tab.
2. You'll see three figures at a glance:
   - **Bookings this month** — how many booking requests have come in
     since the start of the current month.
   - **Total donations** — the running total across all donations ever
     received.
   - **Active members** — members whose membership hasn't expired.

## Managing donations

- The public donation page shows a live progress bar toward the current
  campaign's goal — this updates automatically as donations come in, no
  manual entry needed.
- Admins can view the full donation list and export it as a spreadsheet
  (CSV) for record-keeping or reporting to funders — this option is
  available to Admin accounts (not Staff) under the donations section.

## Promoting a staff member

Account roles (Member / Staff / Admin) aren't changeable from the
website itself yet — this is done directly in the Supabase database by
whoever manages the technical side of the project. If you need someone
promoted to Staff or Admin, pass along their registered email address to
your technical contact.

## Membership tiers

Members can be Free, Standard, or Family tier. There's no online
payment yet — if someone upgrades or renews their membership, an admin
updates their tier directly in the database (via your technical
contact) until a payment system is added.

## Getting help

For anything not covered here — a bug, an unexpected error message, or
a feature request — contact your technical support contact with a
screenshot and a description of what you were trying to do.
